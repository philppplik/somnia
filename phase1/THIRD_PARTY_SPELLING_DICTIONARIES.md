# Spelling word lists (Sheets)

Unmodified Hunspell dictionaries under `public/dict`, loaded one language at a time on first use. Each one ships with its source, version and licence text under `public/licenses`.

| Language | Files | Licence used | Source and version | Notice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| English (US) | en-US.dic / .aff | SCOWL licence (permissive) | SCOWL 2020.12.07 | licenses/scowl-en-US-LICENSE.txt |
| Spanish | es.dic / .aff | LGPL 3 or later (offered with GPL 3+ and MPL 1.1+) | RLA-ES 2.9, LibreOffice/dictionaries | licenses/spell-es-NOTICE.txt |
| French | fr.dic / .aff | MPL 2.0 | Dicollecte 7.7, LibreOffice/dictionaries | licenses/spell-fr-NOTICE.txt |
| Portuguese (Brazil) | pt-BR.dic / .aff | LGPL 3 (offered with the MPL) | VERO / Raimundo Moura 3.3.0, LibreOffice/dictionaries | licenses/spell-pt-BR-NOTICE.txt |

Licence texts: `licenses/spell-LGPL-3.0.txt` (with the GPL 3 it refers to) and `licenses/spell-MPL-2.0.txt`.

The files are separate data files, not linked into the code. German is not shipped: the only free German list (igerman98 / frami) is licensed GPL 2 or 3 only.
